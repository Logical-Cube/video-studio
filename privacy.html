Video Studio

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Video Studio is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channels Wizard of AI News and Run It Local. Only the owner signs in to it, with their own Google account. It isn't available to other users.

What Google data it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, for the owner's own channels only:

  • YouTube Analytics (read only): views, average view duration and audience retention for each video.
  • YouTube account access: uploading videos, and setting thumbnails, captions and playlists.
  • Public YouTube data: video titles, view, like and comment counts, and public comments.

How the data is used

To publish the owner's videos, show their performance in a private dashboard, and work out which kinds of videos do well.

Who the data is shared with

Video titles and performance numbers, including views, likes, comment counts and watch-time statistics, are sent to AI model providers (Xiaomi MiMo and Anthropic Claude) to produce insights about what works on the channels. Links to the owner's videos may also be sent to the owner's own Telegram account. The data isn't sold and isn't used for advertising.

Where it's stored and for how long

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private backup storage. Sign-in tokens are kept in a local file on that computer. Data is kept until the owner deletes it, and the owner can delete it at any time.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time on the Google security settings page.

YouTube and Google

Video Studio uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
