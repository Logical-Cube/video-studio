Video Studio

A private tool for making and publishing videos

Video Studio is a personal desktop tool. It isn't offered to the public and there's nothing to sign up for.

The owner of the YouTube channels Wizard of AI News and Run It Local uses Video Studio to plan, produce and publish their own videos.

How it uses Google sign-in

The owner signs in with their own Google account so the tool can upload finished videos, set thumbnails, captions and playlists, and read those channels' own YouTube Analytics. It doesn't access anyone else's account.
